## Day 4 — The Business Name Gut Check

**Vertical:** Business  
**Platform:** Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Stories  
**Objective:** Authority / Saves-Shares

### Post Title
The Business Name Gut Check

### Hook / First Line / On-Screen Text
You know a great business name when you hear one. Here's what makes it stick.

### Caption
A strong business name usually doesn't need a paragraph of defense.

It lands cleanly. It feels like it belongs to the category — or knows exactly how it's bending it. And it gives people something to remember without making them work too hard.

A useful gut check:

**1. Is it easy to say?**  
If people hesitate, they will avoid saying it.

**2. Is it easy to spell?**  
A clever name that no one can type is expensive in ways you won't see right away.

**3. Does it carry meaning without explaining itself?**  
The best names suggest a world. They don't drag the entire pitch deck behind them.

Your business name does not have to describe everything you do. It does have to give people a reason to remember you.

**Finding names isn't the problem. Finding YOUR name is.**

### Reel / TikTok Script
**Voiceover:**
"You can usually feel when a business name works before you can explain why. It sounds clean. It's easy to repeat. It has a little meaning built in, but it doesn't try to carry the whole business plan. The gut check is simple: can people say it, can they spell it, and does it suggest the right world without needing a long explanation?"

**Platform:** Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest

### Post Title
Name DNA: Stripe

### Hook / First Line / On-Screen Text
Name DNA: Why Stripe works as a business name.

### Caption
**Stripe** is a strong name because it does not try to explain payments in the obvious way.

It doesn't say checkout. It doesn't say transaction. It doesn't say finance. That restraint is part of why it works.

**Sound:**  
Short, crisp, one syllable. Easy to say, easy to remember, hard to mumble.

**Shape:**  
The word feels linear and clean. A stripe is a mark, a band, a line of movement. That gives the name visual structure before the brand even appears.

**Signal:**  
It suggests speed, order, and clarity without trapping the company inside one narrow feature.

**What it avoids:**  
No forced tech spelling. No generic "pay" construction. No over-explained benefit.

The name leaves room for the brand to become bigger than the first product.

That's the quiet advantage of a good business name: it points in the right direction without closing the door.

### Reel / TikTok Script
**Voiceover:**
