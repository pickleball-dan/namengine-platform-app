The difference

Good naming isn't a bigger list. It's a better fit.

The problem is not that naming tools suggest too few names. The problem is that most of them make you sort through noise before they understand what you actually want.

Good naming experience

Understand first, then suggest.

  • Fewer, better names: a focused set shaped by your context and taste.
  • Guided discovery: NamEngine asks what matters before it starts recommending.
  • Love / No feedback: every choice teaches the engine what to move toward or away from.
  • Clear reasons: each name is shown with the feeling, fit, and logic behind it.
Bad naming experience

List first, understanding later.

  • Name dump: hundreds of options with no clear starting point.
  • Guesswork: you are forced to judge names before the system understands your taste.
  • Filter fatigue: sliders and categories ask you to solve the naming problem yourself.
  • No reasoning: names appear without a clear explanation of why they fit.

The learning loop

Every reaction makes the next suggestion better.

NamEngine is not generating at random and hoping something lands. Each Love or No response adjusts what it understands about your taste.

Common questions

Things people ask before starting.

Is the first list free?

Yes. No credit card. You get a first preview list at no cost. Access is optional — you only pay when you want to go deeper with full reports, reactions, and refined rounds.

What does $9.99 include?

Full reports on every name, Love / No reactions that shape the next round, smarter follow-up lists, side-by-side compare, a clear leading choice, and a shareable view — for that vertical, tied to the list you already generated.

Do I pay again for baby and business?

Yes. Access is per vertical. Baby, pet, and business are each separate. If you need more than one, a bundle is the honest next step — not here yet, but that is where it goes.

Do you check if a business name is available?

Reports include practical fit notes. They are not a legal clearance, trademark opinion, or confirmation that a domain is registrable. Always verify independently before you commit.

Can two people use it together?

Yes. After Access is unlocked, share a view with a partner, co-founder, or family member. They see the same shortlist and the same reasoning — without starting over from scratch.

Will NamEngine invent names?

Sometimes. A suggestion may be rare or constructed rather than established. The report will say when a name is coined versus traditional. If something looks unfamiliar, that note will be there.

What if nothing fits?

React honestly and run another round shaped by those reactions. If it still does not feel useful, request a refund — the money-back guarantee is real. Email support@nam-engine.com.
